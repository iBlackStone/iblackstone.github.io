# Acknowledgments

Knit & Crochet has grown through real suggestions, testing, and encouragement. Thank you to everyone who uses the app, reports problems, shares ideas, and gives each improvement time to mature.

## Special acknowledgment

If you use Xiaohongshu and enjoy knitting or crochet, come and connect with us there.

Special thanks to the Xiaohongshu knitting creator **家织婆A-Lin** ([@家织婆A-Lin🥕🌿](https://xhslink.cn/m/9nVeG2bAthU)). Their posts have received 87,000 likes and saves on Xiaohongshu. If you also enjoy knitting or crochet, [visit their profile](https://xhslink.cn/m/9nVeG2bAthU) and connect with this thoughtful and dedicated maker.

Many important parts of Knit & Crochet were born and grew through their long-term, hands-on help. They did more than suggest an idea: they tested it in real use, shared careful feedback, and helped refine it. From asking whether a feature was truly needed, to considering how it could feel easier to use, and how knitting knowledge could be expressed more accurately, each conversation brought the app closer to real crafting workflows.

### The birth of Project Notes

Project Notes grew from their need for a place to record real work and their continued use of it. Reading patterns, recording thoughts, organizing progress, and following a project in practice showed us that Project Notes should be a natural part of the knitting workflow, not merely an attachment to a PDF.

### The birth of the Ravelry section

The Ravelry section grew from their real-world use and continued guidance. Their feedback on finding resources, browsing patterns, organizing projects, and importing content helped Ravelry become a content entry point that fits naturally into a personal knitting workflow.

### The major Yarn Inventory upgrade

The major Yarn Inventory improvements were shaped by their suggestions about interaction details and everyday habits. Their feedback on organizing information, making frequent actions quicker, and keeping lists easier to search and maintain helped Yarn Inventory grow from a simple record into a workspace for daily management.

### The birth of the Pattern Draft editor

During the design of the Pattern Draft editor, they shared valuable knitting knowledge and helped us understand the relationships between concepts and the language of consistent standards. Measurements, components, structure, and calculation logic became clearer through these exchanges, giving the editor a stronger foundation as a reliable creative tool.

### Improving the Knitting editor experience

Many improvements to the Knitting editor experience came from their long-term use and careful observations. Their suggestions, always grounded in real crafting situations, helped us find subtle details in canvas operation, chart reading, and editing that could otherwise easily be missed.

This page is dedicated to thanking them for their continued trust, use, feedback, and companionship. Many suggestions that seemed small at first became another step forward for the app.

## Thank you to our users

Thank you to everyone who helps us find problems, describe real workflows, test new features, and suggest improvements. Every piece of feedback brings the app closer to the way people actually knit and crochet.

## Translation and content help

Thank you to the people who review translations, discuss terminology, and help organize knitting and crochet content. Different languages and crafting traditions help the app serve more creators.

## The open-source community

This app uses many excellent open-source projects, system frameworks, fonts, and tools. We are grateful to their maintainers for sharing knowledge, fixing problems, and building an open development ecosystem.

## Want to help?

If you have sent feedback, joined testing, or would like to help improve Knit & Crochet, please contact us through Feedback in the app. Thank you for helping make the app closer to real creative work.
