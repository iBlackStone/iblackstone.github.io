## Create

Pattern Design includes New, Records, My Templates, Presets, Gauge Library, People, and Common Settings. Free Draw, Strategy, and Template all create the same project type. Built-ins include a basic vest and a drop-shoulder pullover.

<a id="canvas"></a>
## Canvas

The workspace is black and uses centimeter document coordinates. Drag to pan and pinch to zoom. Outlines, grids, dimensions, and shaping steps remain vector-based. Select a piece to edit nodes; lock it to prevent accidental changes.

<a id="tools"></a>
## Tools

The lower toolbar selects Select, Add, Geometry, Structure, Dimensions, Pieces, or Layers; the upper row changes with the mode. Touch and hold for help. A compact toolbar also appears near the selected piece.

You can add FlatPiece, Tube, Band, FreeformPiece, text, and guides; assign knitting semantics to boundaries; and create Join, Split, and Mirror relations.

<a id="counts"></a>
## Dimensions and stitch plan

Enter both measured swatch gauges to generate stitch and row candidates. Plan opens detailed row operations and notation such as `2-1-3`; editing the notation rebuilds the matching detailed steps.

<a id="evidence"></a>
## Evidence and connections

`confirmed` has explicit support, `candidate` needs review, and `unknown` means knowledge is insufficient. Free geometry remains editable without invented algorithms. Counter, knitting-chart, crochet-chart, and yarn-estimate entry points are reserved for later adapters.
