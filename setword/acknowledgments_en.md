Thank you to everyone who uses Knit & Crochet, reports problems, shares ideas, tests new features, and patiently waits for improvements. Your real-world use and feedback help us find problems, understand practical crafting needs, and make the app better step by step.

They did more than suggest ideas—they repeatedly tested, shared feedback, and helped improve the app in real use.

This page is dedicated to thanking 家织婆A-Lin for their continued trust, use, and support. Many suggestions that seemed small at first became another step forward for the app.
